## Suggested starting timing (30 fps, adjustable)

- Idle: 60-90 frames looping.
- Attack: 24-30 frames; anticipation, fast strike, recovery.
- PowerAttack: 42-54 frames; long windup, fast strike, heavy recovery.
- Dodge: 18-24 frames; compress, lateral displacement, reset.
- Hit: 18-24 frames; recoil, stagger, recovery.
